He found the chip in a dead man's boot. Not a processor, not memory. Something older. Something that hummed when the wind hit it right.

The traders in Shanty Row wouldn't touch it. "Bad luck," they said, spitting three times. But Kael knew luck was just another word for information you didn't have yet.

Three days later, the chip led him to a bunker door under the old transit hub. The rust on the handle was fake. Someone had been keeping it clean.

Inside, the air tasted like ozone and stale coffee. A single terminal flickered awake as he stepped closer. The screen displayed one line:

> **WELCOME BACK, JUNKLAND.**

Kael had never been here before. But something in the room recognized him anyway.
